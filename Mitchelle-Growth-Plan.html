The plan

Four phases, built in order.

Each phase builds on the one before it — foundation first, then visibility, then leads, then scale.

01Month 1

Foundation

Make the business significantly easier to trust and book.

  • Conversion-focused website & quote flow
  • Before/after proof, testimonials, brand story
  • Google Business Profile optimization
  • Lead tracking & analytics set up
Result: a site that answers who you are, what you clean, why to trust you, and how to get a quote — fast.
02Month 2

Local Growth

Get in front of more people already searching for cleaning services.

  • Local SEO & service-area pages
  • Google Business Profile content & reviews
  • Reusable content templates for social
Result: more visibility for "house cleaning," "deep cleaning," "office cleaning near me," and similar searches.
03Month 3

Lead Engine

Turn visibility into measurable leads and bookings.

  • Dedicated landing pages & lead tracking
  • Follow-up & reactivation workflow
  • Referral system & conversion optimization
Result: we can finally answer "which channel produces the most bookings?" — not just "are we busier?"
04Month 4–12+

Scale

Once the foundation works, increase volume deliberately.

  • Weekly/biweekly recurring residential growth
  • Realtor, property manager & commercial partnerships
  • Automation, CRM, and SOPs as volume grows
Result: commercial cleaning becomes its own acquisition engine, not just a side hope.
