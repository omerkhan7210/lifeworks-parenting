What I found when I checked
Your business doesn't show up in any Google-connected listings — meaning there's likely no active Google Business Profile. That's probably the single biggest reason people who search for a cleaner in your area aren't finding you. It's also free to fix, which is exactly where Phase 1 starts.
The plan

Three small phases, not one big project

Each phase shows what you'd need to do on your own if you went at this alone, and what I'd take off your plate. Costs stay small and grow only when there's proof it's working.

Phase 1 Get found Weeks 1–4 Lowest cost

Goal: make sure people who search Google or Facebook for a cleaner near them can actually find you, and trust what they see.

If you did it yourself
  • Claim and fill out Google Business Profile
  • Swap stock photos on your site for real ones
  • Add a "book now" line to Facebook posts
What I'd handle
  • Set up and fully complete your Google Business Profile
  • Rebuild your website (moving off Wix to WordPress) with your real photos and a clear quote form
  • Clean up Facebook so every post points toward a booking
✅By end of Phase 1: you show up on Google, your site actually represents your work, and every lead has an easy way to reach you.
Phase 2 Stay visible, build trust Months 2–4 Small monthly step-up

Goal: show up consistently, not just once — and start collecting the reviews that make strangers trust you before they call.

If you did it yourself
  • Post weekly on Google, Facebook, Instagram
  • Text every customer for a review
  • Learn and apply basic SEO to your site
What I'd handle
  • Weekly content: graphics, reels, real job videos, testimonial posts
  • SEO on your website, run for a real 3–4 month stretch (this doesn't work overnight)
  • Automatic review requests after every job
  • Short YouTube videos reusing the same footage — no extra filming
✅By end of Phase 2: you're ranking higher on Google, posting consistently without lifting a finger, and building a real base of reviews.
Phase 3 Add paid growth — only once it's proven Month 4+ Optional, data-backed

Goal: put a little paid budget behind what's already working, instead of guessing where to spend it.

If you did it yourself
  • Try boosting posts and hope for the best
  • No clear way to know what's actually bringing bookings
What I'd handle
  • Small, targeted Facebook/Instagram ad budget behind your best-performing content
  • Track exactly what each dollar brings back before spending more
  • Launch a simple referral offer for existing customers
✅By end of Phase 3: steady bookings from search, social, and referrals — with real numbers behind every dollar spent, so growing the budget is a safe decision, not a leap of faith.
Why phase by phase

Why not just do it all at once?

Small spend to start
Phase 1 is nearly free — it's mostly setup and fixing what's already broken. You're not asked to commit real money until you've already seen movement.
Every phase pays for the next
Getting found (Phase 1) is what makes content and reviews (Phase 2) worth doing. Phase 2 is what makes paid ads (Phase 3) actually work instead of wasting money.
Nothing wasted on ads too early
Spending on ads before your site and Google listing are ready is the most common way small cleaning businesses waste money. This order avoids that.
You stay in control
After each phase, you decide whether to continue based on real results — not a long contract signed on day one.
