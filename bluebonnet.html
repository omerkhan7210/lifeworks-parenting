Built on hard work, rooted in family

Named after a grandmother's favorite flower.

Martin Salinas started Bluebonnet Cleaning Services in July 2019 with a simple vision: provide a straightforward, honest service at a fair price. He reached out to his first client and hasn't stopped since.

The name is a tribute to his grandmother, Flora Eureste — the bluebonnet, Texas's state flower, was her favorite. She was a cleaning woman herself, and Martin has carried that family tradition forward, keeping the business simple, local, and grass-roots as it's grown from Austin into the greater San Antonio area.
