Featured Programs

Programs for every stage of leadership.

From your first ribbon cutting to a seat on the board, the Chamber has a program built for where you are.

Leadership Leander

A nine-month leadership series with hands-on tours and Leander’s best local speakers.

Ambassador Program

Chamber volunteers who welcome new members and show up at every ribbon cutting.

Lunch & Learns

Monthly business education and networking, held the fourth Tuesday of every month.

Chamber Micro Groups

Small, focused peer groups built for referrals and closer business relationships.

Mayor’s Power Hour

An inside look at City Hall priorities, held every other month at Pat Bryson.

Women’s Network

Honoring and connecting Leander’s women in business, anchored by the annual Women of WilCo luncheon.
