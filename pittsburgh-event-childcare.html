Why Families Book Us

The kids' table shouldn't be an afterthought.

Weddings and events are stressful enough without wondering whether your out-of-town family will even show up because they can't find a sitter. We remove that barrier entirely, right on site, so nobody has to choose between celebrating with you and caring for their kids.

From a full pop-up play area with bounce houses and ball pits, to quiet corners for the little ones who need a nap, we plan every setup around the actual kids on your guest list.
