How It Works

From dull to done, dust-free.

Most hardwood projects are completed in a single day, with a dust-free process from start to finish.

01

Assess

We evaluate your floor's material and condition to recommend a cleaning, screen & recoat, or full restoration.

02

Prepare

The surface is screened to remove the worn top layer — or, for deeper damage, sanded down to bare wood.

03

Restore

We apply the right treatment for your floor type: recoating, staining, stripping, waxing or sealing.

04

Reveal

Your floor is left clean, protected and shining — in matte, satin or gloss, your choice.
