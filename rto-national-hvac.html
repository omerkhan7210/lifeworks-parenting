How it works

From application to a working system, in four steps.

RTO National doesn't install your HVAC system. We provide the payment plan behind it, arranged through the independent dealer who does the work.

01

Talk to a participating dealer

Get a quote from a local HVAC dealer in the RTO National network, or tell us your area and we'll point you to one.

02

Choose Lease or Finance

Compare the no-credit-check lease against the $0-down finance option and pick the one that fits your situation.

03

Get an instant decision

Apply online or in person and get an approval decision immediately, before the dealer schedules install day.

04

Install, then pay monthly

Your dealer installs the system on schedule. You make fixed monthly payments directly to RTO National.
