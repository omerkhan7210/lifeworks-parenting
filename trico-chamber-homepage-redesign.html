Why businesses join the Chamber

The Chamber puts our partners in the spotlight — sharing your news, promoting your events, and amplifying your message to the business community and visitors to our area.

Get Connected

Real relationships, regularly.

Morning Mingles, MidDay Mindsets and monthly luncheons keep you face-to-face with the businesses and leaders around you — not just once a year.

Get Seen

Promotion that reaches your area.

From the member directory to event spotlights, TriCo helps put your business in front of the community and the visitors passing through it.

Grow

TriCo U & practical tools.

TriCo U is our business and community university — professional development, networking and practical tools built for members at every stage.

Have a Voice

A seat with city leadership.

Through events like the Elected Officials Reception and standing partnerships with Universal City, Live Oak, Schertz, Selma and Garden Ridge, members stay connected to the people shaping the region.

Fifty years of building the Tri-County community.

Since 1975
Founded as the Randolph Metrocom Chamber, serving the community around Joint Base San Antonio–Randolph.
Since 2014
Renamed the Tri-County Chamber, reflecting a broader regional partnership.
City Partners
Universal City, Live Oak, Schertz, Selma and Garden Ridge all partner directly with the Chamber.
The Vision
To build opportunities with a strong foundation and foster economic prosperity for Texas tri-county communities.
