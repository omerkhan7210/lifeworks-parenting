Who we are

A gallery for
the makers next door.

Up North Gallery is a gift shop that features handmade, local & Minnesota-made goods, plus fun and unique home decor. We're right on Highway 72 — you can't miss us.

Every visit is a little different — new pieces from northwoods makers come through the door often, so there's always something worth stopping in for. Gift cards are always available too, for whenever the right piece finds you.
